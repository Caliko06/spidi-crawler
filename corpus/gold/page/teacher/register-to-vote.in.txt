DOCUMENT: Register to vote

§0 (under the title)
S0 You’ll be asked for your National Insurance number (but you can still register if you do not have one).
S1 Find your National Insurance number.

§1 Who can register to vote
S2 You can register to vote up to 2 years before you reach voting age - check the rules around voting in the UK.

§2 If you live in England or Northern Ireland
S3 You must be aged 16 or over to register.
S4 You can register to vote if you’re a British citizen or an Irish citizen.
S5 You can also register if you have permission (or do not need permission) to enter or stay in the UK, Channel Islands or Isle of Man and you’re:
S6 a Commonwealth citizen (including citizens of Cyprus and Malta)
S7 a citizen of Denmark, Luxembourg, Poland, Portugal or Spain
S8 a citizen of another EU country, who on or before 31 December 2020 had permission to enter or stay (or did not need permission) and this has continued without a break

§3 If you live in Scotland or Wales
S9 You must be aged 14 or over to register.
S10 You can register to vote if you’re a British citizen or an Irish citizen.
S11 You can also register if you have permission to enter or stay in the UK, Channel Islands or Isle of Man, or you do not need permission.

§4 If you live abroad
S12 You can register as an overseas voter if you’ve previously lived in the UK and are either:
S13 a British citizen
S14 an eligible Irish citizen registering to vote in Northern Ireland
S15 You’ll need to give the postcode of the last UK address you were registered to vote at.
S16 If you’ve never registered to vote, you’ll need to give the postcode of the last UK address you lived at.
S17 Contact your electoral registration office if you cannot remember which address you were last registered at.
S18 You may also be asked for your passport details.
S19 There’s a different way to register as an overseas voter if you’re:
S20 registering to vote in Northern Ireland
S21 registering as a Crown servant or British council employee
S22 registering as a member of the armed forces
S23 Find out more about voting if you move or live abroad.

§5 Register using a paper form
S24 You can register using a paper form in England, Wales and Scotland.
S25 You’ll need to print, fill in and send the form to your electoral registration office.

§6 Get help registering
S26 You can get help registering from your electoral registration office.
S27 There’s an easy read guide about registering to vote.

§7 After you’ve registered
S28 Your name and address will appear on the electoral register.
S29 It will also appear on the ‘open’ register unless you asked to opt out when registering.
S30 You can then:
S31 vote in person
S32 apply for a postal vote
S33 apply for a proxy vote
