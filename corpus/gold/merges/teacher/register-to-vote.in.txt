DOCUMENT: Register to vote


THEME "Register to vote"
  s0 [LEAD] You can register to vote if you’re a British citizen or an Irish citizen.

THEME "Register using a paper form"
  s1 You can register using a paper form in England, Wales and Scotland.
  s2 You’ll need to print, fill in and send the form to your electoral registration office.

THEME "After you’ve registered"
  s3 Your name and address will appear on the electoral register.
  s4 It will also appear on the ‘open’ register unless you asked to opt out when registering.

THEME "Who can register to vote"
  s5 You can register to vote up to 2 years before you reach voting age - check the rules around voting in the UK.

SUBJECT "If you live in England or Northern Ireland" (under theme "Who can register to vote")
  s6 You must be aged 16 or over to register.
  s7 [ESSENTIAL] You can register to vote if you’re a British citizen or an Irish citizen.
  s8 You can also register if you have permission (or do not need permission) to enter or stay in the UK, Channel Islands or Isle of Man and you’re:
  s9 a Commonwealth citizen (including citizens of Cyprus and Malta)
  s10 a citizen of Denmark, Luxembourg, Poland, Portugal or Spain
  s11 a citizen of another EU country, who on or before 31 December 2020 had permission to enter or stay (or did not need permission) and this has continued without a break

SUBJECT "If you live abroad" (under theme "Who can register to vote")
  s12 You can register as an overseas voter if you’ve previously lived in the UK and are either:
  s13 a British citizen
  s14 an eligible Irish citizen registering to vote in Northern Ireland
  s15 You’ll need to give the postcode of the last UK address you were registered to vote at.
  s16 If you’ve never registered to vote, you’ll need to give the postcode of the last UK address you lived at.

SUBJECT "If you live in Scotland or Wales" (under theme "Who can register to vote")
  s17 You must be aged 14 or over to register.
  s18 You can also register if you have permission to enter or stay in the UK, Channel Islands or Isle of Man, or you do not need permission.

THEME "Get help registering"
  s19 You can get help registering from your electoral registration office.
