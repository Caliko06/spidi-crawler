§1 step 1
S16 Put 100g plain flour, 2 large eggs, 300ml milk, 1 tbsp sunflower or vegetable oil and a pinch of salt into a bowl or large jug, then whisk to a smooth batter.
S17 This should be similar in consistency to single cream.

§2 step 2
S18 Set aside for 30 mins to rest if you have time, or start cooking straight away.

§3 step 3
S19 Set a medium frying pan or crêpe pan over a medium heat and carefully wipe it with some oiled kitchen paper.

§4 step 4
S20 When hot, cook your pancakes for 1 min on each side until golden, using around half a ladleful of batter per pancake.
S21 Keep them warm in a low oven as you make the rest.

§5 step 5
S22 Serve with lemon wedges and caster sugar, or your favourite filling.
S23 Once cold, you can layer the pancakes between baking parchment, then wrap in cling film and freeze for up to two months.

§6 What is the best pan to make these pancakes?
S24 For crêpes, you can use a non-stick frying pan, if you have one with sloped sides, that's good for sliding them out once cooked.
S25 A seasoned cast iron pan will work well for both American style and crêpes.
S26 Avoid stainless steel frying pans, as if you are not familiar cooking in them, the risk of sticking is quite high.

§7 What should I do if the batter seems too thin/runny?
S27 This classic pancake batter is supposed to be thin, like the consistency of single cream.
S28 However, if you’re looking for a slightly thicker crêpe batter then try our classic crêpes.
S29 For a much thicker and fluffy consistency, make our American pancakes recipe.

§8 How much batter should I use per pancake?
S30 For crêpes, use a ladle and pour enough into the pan, swirling it so that it just coats the bottom, if you are using a large pan, you can double the recipe.
S31 To make 12 crêpes as above, you will want to use a 18cm frying pan, measured over the base.
S32 For American-style use 2-3 tbsp of batter per pancake.

§9 My pancakes are not cooking in the middle
S33 You may be using too much batter for each pancake.
S34 Aim for a thin layer on the bottom of the pan to ensure it cooks evenly.

§10 My batter is quite lumpy, how can I make it smoother?
S35 Prevent lumps forming by slowly whisking the liquid into the flour.
S36 If you do get some lumps, whizz it in a food processor.
